Personal Google integration

Kash Prime

A private assistant for its owner, operated by Kash Farhadi.

What it does

Kash Prime uses Hermes Agent to help its owner through a private Discord conversation and a private browser dashboard. Its Google integration reads the assistant's dedicated Gmail mailbox, sends plain-text emails only to the owner's fixed address, and reads YouTube account and video information.

Google access is authorized by the account owner. The integration does not upload YouTube videos, post comments, change subscriptions, or offer public sign-ups. It is not a Google or Discord product.

Google data

The assistant accesses Gmail messages to answer the owner's requests and prepare updates. YouTube access supports searches and reading channel, subscription and playlist information. Relevant results may be processed by the configured AI provider and shown to the owner in Discord. See the privacy policy for storage, sharing and deletion details.
