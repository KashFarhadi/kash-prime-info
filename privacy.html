Kash Prime / Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective September 5, 2026. This policy describes the private assistant operated by Kash Farhadi.

Data accessed and why

With the account owner's OAuth consent, the integration reads the dedicated Gmail account's profile and messages, including message headers and content. It can send plain-text email to one fixed owner address. It reads and can create or update files in that same dedicated account's Google Drive, Docs and Sheets, and can share those files only with the owner. It reads YouTube channel, subscription, playlist and video metadata to answer requests and find relevant videos. OAuth scopes are Gmail read-only, Gmail send, YouTube read-only, Drive, Docs and Sheets. It has no Google Calendar, Contacts, or public-signup authorization, and it does not use the owner's personal Drive except when a file from the assistant account is shared to the owner.

Use and sharing

Google data is used to carry out the owner's requests and provide assistant responses. Relevant message contents, metadata or YouTube results may be included in requests to the configured OpenAI model service and in responses delivered through Discord or the private dashboard. Those services process data under their applicable terms and privacy policies. This deployment does not promise that Google data remains exclusively on the local machine.

The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop or train generalized AI models. Any human review is limited to the operator for the requested personal workflow, maintenance and security. Kash Prime's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and protection

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in a restricted service account on the owner's Linux virtual machine. The assistant runtime cannot read those credential files. A separate service fixes the email recipient and Drive-share destination, and exposes only the permitted Gmail, YouTube, Drive, Docs and Sheets operations. Credentials are not published on this website or placed in Discord.

Conversation history and tool results may be retained in the assistant's local state, private recovery backups, and Discord history. Access is restricted to the owner and authorized administration. Local backups use filesystem access restrictions; the archive format itself does not provide encryption. GitHub hosts this informational website and may collect technical visitor information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

Retention, control and deletion

The operator retains data while needed for the personal workflow and recovery, until it is deleted. There is no automatic deletion schedule currently configured. The account owner can revoke authorization in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not itself erase existing messages, conversation records or backups. Contact the operator to delete local credentials, retained assistant records and applicable backups. Discord and AI-provider records remain subject to those providers' retention and deletion controls.

Contact and changes

Questions or deletion requests: Kash Farhadi at kashprimeai@gmail.com. This page will be updated when the integration's data practices change.
